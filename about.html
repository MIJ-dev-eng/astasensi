ASTASENSI / PLAYER GUIDE

About AstaSensi

AstaSensi is a free, browser-based helper designed to give Free Fire players a practical starting point for tuning their sensitivity settings around their phone and play style.

Our goal

We want to make it easier for players to begin experimenting with sensitivity without needing to create an account, install an app, or pay for a settings guide. You can enter your device details, choose your preferred play style, and generate a suggested profile when you are ready.

How our suggestions work

The generator uses a combination of device-related starter profiles and general rules based on the options you select. Not every phone has been individually tested, so results are suggestions—not laboratory measurements, official settings, or a promise of headshots.

Independent fan project

AstaSensi is an independent fan-made project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Garena or the Free Fire brand owners.

Our approach: start with a sensible baseline, test it in Training Mode, and adjust it to your own touch response and comfort.
