    THE BASICS

    Understanding Free Fire sensitivity

    Sensitivity controls how quickly your view or aim responds when you move your finger across the screen. A higher value generally makes the corresponding movement faster, while a lower value can make it slower and more controlled. The best balance depends on your device, grip, HUD layout, and how you aim.

    AstaSensi provides a starting profile based on the options you select. It does not measure your phone's actual touch response or test every setting on every device, so treat the result as a practical starting point—not a guaranteed best configuration.

    General

    Useful for broad camera movement and quick target acquisition. If your view regularly moves past the target, try a small reduction or use a shorter swipe.

    Red Dot

    Influences aiming while using a red-dot sight. Adjust gradually until you can move toward a target without repeatedly overshooting it.

    2x Scope

    Affects movement while aiming with a 2x scope. Focus on tracking targets smoothly and making small corrections.

    4x Scope

    Can affect precision at longer distances. If your crosshair feels unstable, test a slightly lower value and smaller finger movements.

    Sniper Scope

    Helps determine how quickly your aim shifts when using sniper scopes. Controlled adjustments may be more useful than maximum speed.

    Free Look

    Controls how quickly you can look around without directly moving your weapon aim. Choose a value that makes checking your surroundings comfortable.

    Settings and their available ranges may change between game versions. Use the labels and ranges shown in your own game as the final reference.

    CHOOSE A STARTING APPROACH

    Match your settings to your play style

    Drag-headshot players

    If you usually move your finger upward to bring the crosshair toward an opponent's head, focus on repeatable swipes. A setting that is too fast may send your aim above the target; one that is too slow may make you drag farther than intended. Change the relevant control in small steps and repeat the same movement during practice.

    Close-range and rushing players

    Close-range fights often involve fast turns and moving targets. Practise following an opponent while moving, and make sure your fire button is positioned where your finger can reach it comfortably. Sensitivity alone cannot replace movement, timing, or crosshair placement.

    Balanced players

    If you use several weapon types, aim for a profile that feels predictable across different distances. Avoid making one control extremely fast just to improve a single situation if it makes other situations difficult to manage.

    Scoped and longer-range players

    Long-range aiming often rewards smaller corrections. Test scoped controls separately from general camera movement, and avoid changing multiple scope values at the same time.

    TESTING METHOD

    How to test and fine-tune your generated profile

    1. Record your current setup. Take a screenshot of your existing settings and HUD before changing them. This gives you a simple way to restore your previous configuration.
    2. Generate a starting profile. Select your phone brand, model, performance estimate, play style, and usual swipe length as accurately as you can.
    3. Enter the values carefully. Compare each value with the corresponding control in the game. Do not assume a setting was applied correctly without checking it.
    4. Use Training Mode first. Practise the same movement several times against stationary targets before judging whether the profile feels comfortable.
    5. Identify one specific issue. Decide whether your aim is overshooting, stopping short, shaking, or feeling slow. A clear observation is more useful than changing values at random.
    6. Change one setting at a time. Make a small adjustment, repeat the same test, and compare the result. This helps you understand which change made a difference.
    7. Practise before deciding. Give a profile more than a few attempts. Constantly switching settings can make it harder to build consistent muscle memory.

    DEVICE GUIDE

    How your phone and controls can affect aiming

    Two players can use the same sensitivity values and still experience different results. Screen dimensions, touch response, frame-rate consistency, temperature, grip, and button placement all influence how a swipe feels. The device information entered into this generator is used to create a suggested profile; it is not a live hardware test.

    • Performance stability: If the game stutters, try graphics settings your phone can maintain consistently and close unnecessary background apps before testing again.
    • Heat: If your device becomes hot and performance changes during a session, let it cool and compare settings under similar conditions.
    • Screen size and grip: A larger or smaller screen can change how far your finger naturally travels. Hold the phone in your usual playing position while testing.
    • Touch surface: A clean, dry screen can help make finger movement more consistent. If you use a screen protector, consider whether it changes how your swipes feel.
    • HUD layout: Fire-button size, button placement, and the distance between controls can affect your drag technique. Make sure the layout is comfortable before repeatedly changing sensitivity.
    • Game updates: After an update, check the in-game controls and available sensitivity ranges rather than assuming all previous values and behaviour remain identical.

    TROUBLESHOOTING

    Common aiming problems and what to try

    My aim goes above the target

    Your swipe may be too long or the relevant sensitivity may feel too fast for your current technique. Try a shorter swipe first. If the problem continues, lower the relevant value slightly and repeat the same test.

    My aim stops below the target

    You may be moving your finger too little, or the setting may feel too slow. Try a more complete but controlled swipe. If needed, increase the relevant value in a small step rather than changing the whole profile.

    My crosshair shakes at longer range

    Try smaller finger movements and test the relevant scoped control separately. Also check whether performance drops, an uncomfortable grip, or accidental contact with nearby buttons is affecting your aim.

    My settings feel different from one session to another

    Check whether you changed your HUD, game settings, device performance conditions, or grip. Compare settings under similar conditions and keep a screenshot of a profile that feels reliable.

    The generated profile does not feel right

    That is normal: the profile is a starting suggestion, not a measurement of your personal aim. Use your own training results to make small changes, or regenerate a profile with more accurate device and play-style choices.

    PRACTICE PLAN

    A simple 10-minute aiming routine

    Minutes 1–2

    Warm up

    Move your view between targets at a comfortable speed. Focus on control rather than trying to move as quickly as possible.

    Minutes 3–5

    Practise drag control

    Repeat short and medium upward swipes. Notice whether your crosshair consistently stops short or travels too far.

    Minutes 6–7

    Track moving targets

    Practise following movement while keeping your crosshair under control. Avoid changing settings during this test.

    Minutes 8–9

    Test your scopes

    Try the scoped controls you use most often. Make smaller adjustments when aiming at targets farther away.

    Minute 10

    Review your results

    Identify one repeatable problem and write down any adjustment you want to test in your next practice session.

    This is a suggested practice routine, not a promise of headshots, wins, or rank improvement. Progress depends on practice, game conditions, and individual technique.

    TRANSPARENCY

    What AstaSensi does—and does not do

    AstaSensi is an independent browser-based helper for creating a starting sensitivity profile from the choices you provide. It is intended to make it easier to begin testing a setup and understand how to tune it for your own preferences.

    • It suggests starting values based on the options selected in the form.
    • It does not connect to your game account, inspect your phone's hardware, or change in-game settings automatically.
    • It does not guarantee headshots, wins, better rank, or a perfect profile for every device or player.
    • It cannot account for every difference in game version, touch response, HUD layout, network conditions, or personal technique.
    • Its suggestions should be checked in the game and adjusted to match the controls and ranges available in your current version.

    Yes. The generator is presented as a free tool and does not require you to create an account to generate a starting profile.

    Will these settings guarantee headshots?

    No. Sensitivity is only one part of aiming. Practice, timing, movement, crosshair placement, HUD layout, and device performance also matter.

    Can I use the same settings on every phone?

    You can test the same values on different devices, but they may not feel the same. Screen size, touch response, performance, and personal grip can affect the result.

    Should I change my settings after every match?

    Usually not. Frequent changes can make it difficult to develop consistent muscle memory. Practise with one profile and change a value only when you have identified a specific issue.

    Does AstaSensi automatically change my game settings?

    No. It provides suggested values for you to review and enter manually in the game.
