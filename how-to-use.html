How to use AstaSensi

Follow these steps to generate a sensitivity profile for your device.

1. Choose your phone brand

Select the brand that made your phone. If you do not see it, choose “Other brand.”

2. Enter your exact model

Type the model name shown in your phone settings or on the device box. For example, you can enter “Redmi A5.” The model list is only a suggestion list; you can type a model that is not listed.

3. Set performance and play style

Choose “Estimate for me” if you are unsure about performance. Select the style that best matches how you play: drag headshots, close-range/rush, balanced, or scoped/long-range.

4. Choose your swipe length

Use short for small controlled movements, medium for a balanced movement, or long for a faster upward drag.

5. Generate your profile

Press Generate My Sensitivity. Your settings will appear only after you submit the form. You can then copy the values or regenerate a profile.

6. Test and adjust

  1. Open Free Fire and enter Training Mode.
  2. Test your usual weapons and movement.
  3. If your aim passes over the target, lower the relevant sensitivity slightly.
  4. If your aim feels slow, increase it in small steps.
  5. Change one or two values at a time so you can tell what helped.

There is no universal sensitivity that guarantees headshots. Screen size, frame rate, touch response, HUD layout, game updates, and personal technique all affect the result.
