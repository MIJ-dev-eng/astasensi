Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AstaSensi handles information when you use the AstaSensi website.

Information you enter into the generator

The current sensitivity generator runs in your browser. The phone brand, model, performance selection, play style, and swipe preference you enter are used locally to calculate and display suggested values. The site does not currently send these form choices to an AstaSensi server or require an account.

Technical information and hosting

Like most websites, the hosting provider or infrastructure used to serve this site may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, timestamps, and security logs. The exact information retained depends on the hosting provider and its settings.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The current version of AstaSensi does not intentionally set its own analytics or advertising cookies. If analytics, advertising services such as Google AdSense, embedded content, or other third-party services are added later, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. This policy should be updated before or when those services are enabled.

External links and third parties

The site may link to third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before sharing personal information with them.

Children's privacy

The site is a general-purpose gaming tool and is not designed to request personal information from children. Do not send sensitive personal information through email.

Your choices

You can choose not to use the generator. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings, though disabling certain browser features may affect functionality.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the website or its services change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when we make material changes.

Contact
