Frequently asked questions

Is AstaSensi free?

Yes. The sensitivity generator is free to use and does not require an account.

Will these settings guarantee headshots?

No. The values are starting suggestions, not guarantees. Your technique, HUD, frame rate, touch response, and game version affect your aim.

Are all phone models tested?

No. Some devices may have starter profiles, while other models use general rules to estimate values. We do not claim every model has been individually tested.

Why isn't my exact phone listed?

Type the full model name manually. The model suggestions do not limit which model you can enter.

Does the tool change my game settings automatically?

No. It only displays values for you to enter manually in the game. It does not connect to or modify Free Fire.

Does AstaSensi collect my phone details?

The current generator processes the choices in your browser and does not send them to an AstaSensi server. Hosting providers may still process standard technical logs; see the Privacy Policy.

Can I copy my generated values?

Yes. Use the Copy settings button after generating your profile. If clipboard access is blocked by your browser, you can read and enter the values manually.

Is AstaSensi official?

No. It is an independent fan-made tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Garena.

How can I contact the site?

Visit the Contact Us page to email the site owner.
